NEGATIVE

📋 Inclusion & Stability Criteria

CriterionSpecificationRef
PrimaryHF hospitalization within 30 days[1]
LVEFAny LVEF (70.6% had <40%)[1]
ExclusionsNursing home, survival <6mo, cognitive impairment[1]

🩸 Blood Tests

  • • No BNP/NT-proBNP requirement
  • • 54% had CKD (eGFR <60)
  • • Labs per standard care

✅ Stability Definition

  • • Discharged from HF hospitalization
  • • Able to stand on scale
  • • No planned procedure
  • • No severe cognitive impairment

⚙️ Intervention: WHO does WHAT, HOW OFTEN

👩‍⚕️ SITE COORDINATOR (Nurse)

  • 📊 Data Review: Every weekday (M-F)
  • 📞 Patient Calls: When variances detected
  • 📚 Education: Demonstrate system, HFSA materials
  • ⏰ Reminders: After 2 days non-use

👨‍⚕️ CLINICIAN (Physician)

  • ⚕️ Primary care: Retains responsibility
  • 💊 Medication: Adjustments per symptoms
  • 📋 Documentation: Each variance management

Result: No benefit. 180-day readmission/death: 52.3% vs 51.5% (p=0.75). n=1,653. [1]

4. TELESAT-HF Study ⭐ (EJHeartFail 2025)

Girerd N et al. Eur J Heart Fail. 2025;27:1658-1669

POSITIVE
🔑 Unique: Algorithm-adjusted monitoring frequency. Phone monitoring for digitally illiterate patients.

📋 Inclusion Criteria

CriterionSpecificationRef
PrimaryHospitalized for HF (ICD-10: I50)[4]
SettingFrance, >300 healthcare centers[4]
DigitalBoth digital literate AND illiterate included[4]

⚙️ Intervention: WHO does WHAT, HOW OFTEN

👩‍⚕️ SATELIA NURSES (18→28 staff)

  • 📞 Onboarding: Within 48 hours of inclusion
  • 📊 Phone monitoring: For non-digital patients
  • 📚 Education: 3 sessions/semester (phone)
  • 🔔 Follow-up: Non-responsive patients

👨‍⚕️ CARDIOLOGIST/PHYSICIAN

  • 📋 Alerts: From Satelia® algorithm
  • ⚕️ Decisions: Clinical management
  • 💊 Medication: Adjustments
Patient Education: Daily challenge (2 T/F questions + tip), 5 key areas, 9-week cycle, 40 different questions. [4]
Result: ✓ All-cause mortality HR 0.64 (0.59-0.70, p<0.0001). Less time in hospital (-2.1%). n=18,882. [4]

4. TELESAT-HF Studie ⭐

Frankreich, >300 Zentren. Satelia® Algorithmus. Telefon-Monitoring für digital nicht versierte Patienten. 3 Schulungen/Semester. [4]

4. Étude TELESAT-HF ⭐

France, >300 centres. Algorithme Satelia®. Suivi téléphonique pour patients non numériques. 3 sessions éducatives/semestre. [4]

4. Studio TELESAT-HF ⭐

Francia, >300 centri. Algoritmo Satelia®. Monitoraggio telefonico per pazienti non digitali. 3 sessioni educative/semestre. [4]

5. HerzMobil Tirol (Poelzl) ⭐

Pölzl G et al. Clin Res Cardiol. 2022;111:334-344

POSITIVE
🔑 Key: Multidimensional post-discharge DMP with telemedical monitoring in comprehensive network of HF nurses + physicians.

📋 Inclusion Criteria

CriterionSpecificationRef
PrimaryAcute HF hospitalization (post-discharge)[5]
SettingAustria, Tirol region (2016-2019)[5]
DesignNon-randomized, matched for age and sex[5]

⚙️ Intervention: WHO does WHAT, HOW OFTEN

👩‍⚕️ HF SPECIALIST NURSES

  • 📊 Telemonitoring: Daily vital signs review
  • 📞 Structured calls: Regular patient contact
  • 📚 Education: Self-care, warning signs
  • 🏠 Home visits: Post-discharge assessment

👨‍⚕️ RESIDENT PHYSICIANS + SPECIALISTS

  • 🏥 Network: Secondary & tertiary referral centers
  • 💊 GDMT: Optimization per guidelines
  • ⚕️ Consultation: Complex cases
Network Structure: Comprehensive network of HF nurses, resident physicians, secondary and tertiary referral centres. [5]
Result: ✓ Primary endpoint (6mo HF readmission/death): 19.1% vs 34.6% (HR 0.54, p<0.001). 1-year mortality: 10% vs 25.7% (HR 0.38, p<0.001). n=508. [5]

6. MAR-HF-Titration Study ⭐ (EJCN 2025)

Eur J Cardiovasc Nurs. 2025;zvaf161

POSITIVE
🔑 Focus: Nurse-led telemedicine for FAST quadruple therapy optimization in HFrEF.

📋 Inclusion Criteria

CriterionSpecificationRef
HF TypeHFrEF (reduced ejection fraction)[6]
LVEFMean 31% at baseline[6]
New Diagnosis61% newly diagnosed HF[6]
EnrollmentSept 2021 - Dec 2023, n=210[6]

🩸 Blood Tests & Monitoring

  • • NT-proBNP: 1960→806 pg/mL (p<0.001) post-intervention
  • • Routine lab monitoring during 6-month follow-up
  • • Risk factors for poor titration: CKD, hypotension, higher NT-proBNP, older age

[6] Results

⚙️ Intervention: WHO does WHAT, HOW OFTEN

👩‍⚕️ NURSE-LED PROGRAMME

  • 📊 Daily telemonitoring: Vital signs, weight, symptoms
  • 📹 Videoconferences: Regular patient contact
  • 💊 Drug titration: Using algorithms
  • 🩺 Lab monitoring: Routine during follow-up

👨‍⚕️ PHYSICIAN SUPERVISION

  • 📋 Algorithms: For nurse drug titration
  • ⚕️ Oversight: Complex cases
  • 💊 Prescriptions: Quadruple therapy

MRA65 days
ARNI69 days
Result: ✓ Quadruple therapy: 63%→75% (p=0.001). ≥50% target doses: 10%→44% (p<0.001). LVEF: 31%→45% (p<0.001). [6]
