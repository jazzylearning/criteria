NEGATIVE

  • • Inability to stand on scale

👩‍⚕️ SITE COORDINATOR (Nurse)

📊 Data Review

Review telemonitoring data every weekday (M-F, except holidays)

📞 Patient Contact

Call when variances detected; Call after 2 days non-use

📚 Education

Demonstrate system at enrollment; Provide HFSA materials

👨‍⚕️ CLINICIAN (Physician)

⚕️ Clinical Decisions

Primary responsibility for patient care; Receive variance alerts

💊 Medication

Adjustments based on symptoms; Diuretic changes for weight gain

📋 Documentation

Required to document management of each variance

1. Tele-HF Studie (NEJM 2010)

Hospitalisierung wegen HI innerhalb der letzten 30 Tage. Jede LVEF akzeptiert. Studienkoordinatoren überprüfen Daten jeden Werktag. PHQ-2 Depression-Screening alle 30 Tage. [Ref 1]

3. TIM-HF2 Study ⭐ POSITIVE

Koehler F et al. Lancet. 2018;392:1047-1057

POSITIVE

🔑 KEY LEARNING: Refined patient selection based on TIM-HF post-hoc analysis

Added: Recent hospitalization requirement + Depression exclusion (PHQ-9 >9)

📋 REFINED INCLUSION CRITERIA

CriterionTIM-HF2vs TIM-HFRef
LVEF≤45%Was ≤35%[3]
NYHA ClassII or IIISame[3]
🆕 Recent HF HospitalizationREQUIRED within 12 monthsNEW![3]
🆕 Depression ScreeningPHQ-9 score ≤9 (excludes moderate-severe)NEW![3]

📱 Enhanced Equipment (vs TIM-HF)

3-lead ECG Blood Pressure Weight Scale 🆕 SpO2 (Oxygen Saturation) Tablet PC

👨‍⚕️ TMC PHYSICIAN (Enhanced)

  • ⏰ 24/7 physician-led monitoring
  • ⚕️ Direct treatment authority (not just recommendations)
  • 📋 Structured remote patient management
  • 📞 Monthly structured patient contact

👩‍⚕️ LOCAL CARE TEAM

  • 🤝 Collaborative care with primary physician
  • 💊 Individualized therapy optimization
  • 📚 Education and self-care support
  • 🏥 In-person visits: 3, 6, 9 months

📊 RESULT: POSITIVE ✓

Days lost to unplanned CV hospitalization/death: 4.88% vs 6.64% (p=0.046). All-cause mortality: 7.86% vs 11.34% (HR 0.70, p=0.028). n=1,571. [3]

3. TIM-HF2 Studie ⭐ POSITIV

LVEF ≤45%, NYHA II-III, HI-Hospitalisierung innerhalb 12 Monate, PHQ-9 ≤9. 24/7 ärztliche Überwachung mit direkter Behandlungsautorität. [Ref 3]

📊 ERGEBNIS: POSITIV ✓ - Mortalitätsreduktion: 7,86% vs 11,34%

3. Étude TIM-HF2 ⭐ POSITIVE

FEVG ≤45%, NYHA II-III, hospitalisation IC dans les 12 mois, PHQ-9 ≤9. Surveillance médicale 24h/24 avec autorité de traitement direct. [Réf 3]

3. Studio TIM-HF2 ⭐ POSITIVO

4. TELESAT-HF Study ⭐ POSITIVE

Girerd N et al. Eur J Heart Fail. 2025;27:1658-1669

POSITIVE

📋 INCLUSION CRITERIA

CriterionSpecificationRef
Primary RequirementHospitalized for HF (ICD-10: I50 primary or secondary)[4]
CountryFrance (>300 healthcare centers)[4]
Digital LiteracyBoth digital literate AND illiterate patients included (phone alternative)[4]

🔑 UNIQUE: Algorithm-Adjusted Monitoring Frequency + Digital Divide Solution

Satelia® Cardio algorithm tailors monitoring frequency to patient risk profile. Phone monitoring for digitally illiterate patients.

👩‍⚕️ SATELIA NURSES (18→28 over study)

  • 📞 Onboarding call within 48 hours of inclusion
  • 📊 Phone-based data collection for non-digital patients
  • 📚 3 educational sessions/semester (phone)
  • 🔔 Follow-up calls for non-responsive patients

👨‍⚕️ CARDIOLOGIST/PHYSICIAN

  • 📋 Receives automated alerts from algorithm
  • ⚕️ Clinical decision-making
  • 💊 Medication adjustments
  • 🏥 Prescribes RMP enrollment

📚 Structured Patient Education

Patients self-select expertise level: A (guided), B (eager to learn), C (expert)

Daily Challenge

2 true/false questions + tip

5 Key Areas

Deterioration signs, diet, medications, exercise, weight

Cycle Duration

9 weeks, 40 different questions

📊 RESULT: POSITIVE ✓ (Real-world, n=18,882)

All-cause mortality: HR 0.64 (95% CI 0.59-0.70, p<0.0001). Less time in hospital (-2.1%). Fewer emergency visits (RR 0.83). [4]

4. TELESAT-HF Studie ⭐ POSITIV

Frankreich, >300 Zentren. Satelia® Cardio mit algorithmusangepasster Überwachungsfrequenz. Telefon-Monitoring für digital nicht versierte Patienten. 3 Schulungssitzungen/Semester. [Ref 4]

4. Étude TELESAT-HF ⭐ POSITIVE

France, >300 centres. Satelia® Cardio avec fréquence de surveillance adaptée par algorithme. Suivi téléphonique pour patients non numériques. 3 sessions éducatives/semestre. [Réf 4]

4. Studio TELESAT-HF ⭐ POSITIVO

Francia, >300 centri. Satelia® Cardio con frequenza di monitoraggio adattata dall'algoritmo. Monitoraggio telefonico per pazienti non digitali. 3 sessioni educative/semestre. [Rif 4]

5. Nurse-Led GDMT Optimization

Zheng J et al. J Card Fail. 2023;29(7):1000-1013 (Systematic Review)

POSITIVE

📊 Meta-analysis: 33 studies, 17 RCTs, 5,268 patients

82% of RCTs evaluated nurse interventions; remainder pharmacist-led

📋 TYPICAL INCLUSION ACROSS STUDIES

CriterionRangeRef
EF Restriction71% specified EF ≤40-50%[5]
NYHA Class24% selected NYHA II-IV[5]
Follow-upMedian 6 months (range 1-24)[5]

👩‍⚕️ NURSE ROLE (82% of RCTs)

  • 💊 GDMT Titration:
    • • ACEi/ARB initiation: RR 2.09 (p<0.05)
    • • Beta-blocker initiation: RR 1.91 (p<0.05)
    • • Uptitration to target doses
  • 📋 Practice Scope: 41% had independent prescribing authority
  • 🏥 Setting: 76% outpatient, 24% transitional

👨‍⚕️ PHYSICIAN ROLE

  • ⚕️ Supervision: 59% non-independent nurse practice
  • 📋 Usual Care Providers:
    • • General practice: 61%
    • • Cardiologist: 48%
  • 💊 Approves titration in non-independent models

💊 GDMT Classes in Protocols

ACEi/ARB (82%) Beta-blocker (91%) MRA (42%) ARNI (18%) SGLT2i (3%)

📊 RESULT: Improved GDMT Utilization

RASI initiation: RR 2.09. BB initiation: RR 1.91. Mortality trend: RR 0.82 (0.67-1.04). HF hospitalization trend: RR 0.80 (0.63-1.01). [5]

5. Pflegekraft-geführte GDMT-Optimierung

Meta-Analyse: 33 Studien, 82% mit Pflegekraft-Interventionen. ACEi/ARB-Initiierung: RR 2,09. Betablocker-Initiierung: RR 1,91. [Ref 5]

5. Optimisation GDMT Dirigée par Infirmiers

Méta-analyse: 33 études, 82% interventions infirmières. Initiation IEC/ARA: RR 2,09. Initiation bêtabloquants: RR 1,91. [Réf 5]

5. Ottimizzazione GDMT Guidata da Infermieri

Meta-analisi: 33 studi, 82% interventi infermieristici. Iniziazione ACEi/ARB: RR 2,09. Iniziazione beta-bloccanti: RR 1,91. [Rif 5]
