⚙️ Detailed Intervention Protocol

👩‍⚕️ SITE COORDINATOR (Nurse Role)

📊 Daily Monitoring Tasks
  • • Review telemonitoring data every weekday (except holidays)
  • • Access data via secure Internet site
  • • Identify and document "variances" (alert triggers)

[1] Methods: "reviewed every weekday by site coordinators"

📞 Patient Contact
  • • Contact patients when variances detected
  • • Call non-compliant patients after 2 days of non-use
  • • Provide device troubleshooting support
📚 Education Tasks
  • • Demonstrate telemonitoring system at enrollment
  • • Provide HFSA educational materials
  • • Distribute scales and telephones as needed

👨‍⚕️ CLINICIAN (Physician Role)

⚕️ Clinical Management
  • • Primary responsibility for patient care
  • • Receive variance alerts from coordinators
  • • Make clinical decisions on variances
  • • Implement treatment changes
💊 Medication Decisions
  • • Medication adjustments based on symptoms
  • • Diuretic dose changes for weight gain
  • • GDMT optimization per guidelines

[1] "treat patients in accordance with national guidelines"

📋 Response to Variances
  • • Required to document management of each variance
  • • Education about dietary indiscretions
  • • Referrals for office visits when indicated

⚙️ Detailed Intervention Protocol

👨‍⚕️ TELEMEDICAL CENTER PHYSICIAN

⏰ Availability

24 hours/day, 7 days/week for entire study period

[2] "physician-led medical support 24 hours per day, 7 days per week"

📊 Data Review
  • • Review daily transmitted data
  • • Analyze ECG, BP, weight trends
  • • Identify clinical deterioration
⚕️ Clinical Actions
  • • Verify measurements
  • • Give consultation
  • • Institute treatment directly
  • • Contact local physician

👩‍⚕️ LOCAL PHYSICIAN (Network)

🏥 Primary Responsibility

Retains general responsibility for patient care

[2] "general responsibility for the patient's care remained with the local physician"

  • • Contacted by TMC at least every 3 months
  • • Receives treatment recommendations
  • • Implements long-term changes
💊 Medication Management
  • • GDMT optimization per guidelines
  • • Regular prescription management

🏆 KEY LEARNING FROM TIM-HF: Patient selection is crucial!

TIM-HF2 refined inclusion criteria based on post-hoc analysis of TIM-HF showing benefit in subgroup with recent hospitalization and without depression.

📋 Refined Inclusion & Stability Criteria

Criterion TIM-HF2 (Refined) vs. TIM-HF
LVEF ≤45% Was ≤35%
NYHA Class II or III Same
🆕 Recent Hospitalization REQUIRED: HF hospitalization within 12 months NEW criterion!
🆕 Depression Screening PHQ-9 score ≤9 (excludes moderate-severe depression) NEW criterion!
Medical Therapy On stable heart failure therapy Same

⚠️ Key Exclusion: Depression (PHQ-9 >9)

Post-hoc analysis of TIM-HF showed patients with depression (PHQ-9 >9) did not benefit from telemonitoring. This was incorporated as key exclusion criterion in TIM-HF2. [3]

⚙️ Enhanced Intervention Protocol

👨‍⚕️ TELEMEDICAL CENTER (24/7)

⏰ Enhanced Availability

  • • Direct treatment authority
⚕️ Key Enhancement vs TIM-HF
  • • Can institute treatment directly
  • • Not just recommendations - direct action
  • • Collaborative approach with local MD

📞 Structured Contact
  • • Monthly structured patient contact
  • • Individualized therapy optimization
  • • Education and self-care support

  • • Collaborative care with primary physician

📊 Study Result: POSITIVE ✓

  • • Primary endpoint: % days lost to unplanned CV hospitalization or death: 4.88% vs 6.64% (p=0.046)
  • • All-cause mortality: 7.86% vs 11.34% (HR 0.70, p=0.028)
  • • n=1,571 patients (765 RPM, 773 usual care)
  • • Follow-up: 12 months

[3] "Remote patient management reduced the percentage of days lost to unplanned cardiovascular hospital admissions and all-cause death"

📋 Verfeinerte Einschlusskriterien

LVEF ≤45%, NYHA II-III, HI-Hospitalisierung innerhalb 12 Monaten, PHQ-9 ≤9 (Ausschluss Depression). [Ref 3]
